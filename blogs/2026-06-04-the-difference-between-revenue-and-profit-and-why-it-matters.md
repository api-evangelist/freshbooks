Revenue is the money a business brings in while profit is what remains after paying all bills — two numbers that tell very different stories about financial health. This guide explains how both metrics show up on the books and which one small business owners, from freelance designers to multi-person agencies, should be watching to distinguish a busy month from a genuinely profitable one.
